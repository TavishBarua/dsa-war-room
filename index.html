04 — The Code

RULES OF
ENGAGEMENT

Being a senior engineer means you can learn this fast. Here's the protocol that separates people who crack FAANG from people who just LeetCode.

🧠
UNDERSTAND, DON'T MEMORIZE
After seeing a solution, close it. Wait 5 mins. Now code it from scratch. If you can't, you don't know it. Memorized solutions fail in interviews. Understood patterns don't.
🗣️
TALK WHILE YOU TYPE
FAANG interviewers want to hear your brain work. Practice narrating: "I'm thinking HashMap here because we need O(1) lookup..." Do this even alone. It becomes automatic.
⏱️
THE 15-MINUTE RULE
Stuck for 15 minutes? Look at the approach hint only — not the code. Still stuck after 15 more? Watch the solution. But code it yourself after. Never copy-paste.
📊
ALWAYS STATE BIG-O
Every single solution you write, say the time AND space complexity out loud. If you can't explain it, you can't defend it in an interview. This is non-negotiable at senior level.
🔁
REVISIT AFTER 3 DAYS
Spaced repetition is the secret. After solving a problem, flag it. Come back in 3 days and solve it again cold. If you can — you own it. If not — re-learn it properly.
🎯
2 PROBLEMS MINIMUM DAILY
You're busy. Fine. 2 problems a day is still 150 in 75 days. The streak matters more than the count. Miss one day? Never miss two. Consistency beats intensity every time.
